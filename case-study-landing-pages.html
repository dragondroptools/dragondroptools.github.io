Landing pages for streaming sites
Working on landing pages to increase user sign ups with live campaign materials across the amc+ brand account for Croud.
Key considerations here were ensuring the offer was included clearly above the fold and the highlight content was prominent on the pages. I created card modules for individual titles and proposed updated carousel structure to improve the hierarchy. I also created a proposed design template that can beused by each of AMCs six streaming sites. The purpose of this was to demonstratehow one universal template would look unique when populated with brand assets and different content. Features were added to encourage conversion when unregistered users visit the page.
